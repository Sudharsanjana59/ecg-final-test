How to play

Six steps, one rhythm at a time. Read this once — you can always come back via "ℹ️ How to play" on the levels screen.

🧩
Step 1

Pick a level

Each level is one ECG rhythm, start to finish — Normal Sinus Rhythm, Atrial Fibrillation, and so on. Levels unlock in order as you clear them.

🫀
Step 2

Read the empty strip

The level shows a named ECG strip with a row of empty dashed slots — the waveform is missing and needs to be rebuilt piece by piece.

🖐️
Step 3

Drag the right pieces

Below the strip is a tray of small waveform pieces. Some are correct; others are decoys borrowed from a different rhythm — same size, wrong pattern. Drag each piece into a slot, left to right.

🔁
Step 4

Swap freely

Not sure yet? Drag a placed piece back to the tray, or drop a new one straight on top of it to swap — nothing is locked in until you submit.

✅
Step 5

Submit & check

Once every slot is filled, hit Submit. Each slot lights up green (correct) or red (wrong), and a hint explains the real rhythm if you didn't get it perfect.

⏱️
Step 6

Score & unlock

Your score ticks down every second you're still playing, then multiplies by your accuracy when you submit — fast and correct scores highest. A perfect rebuild unlocks the next level.

🎓 Certificate & leaderboard

Clear every level and a Certificate of Completion unlocks automatically — downloadable as a PNG or printable as a PDF. Every score you submit also lands on the Leaderboard, ranked by total points across every level you've cleared.
